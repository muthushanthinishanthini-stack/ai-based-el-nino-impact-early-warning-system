## Brand & Style

The design system establishes a high-precision, authoritative command interface engineered for scientific computing, climate risk telemetry, and institutional disaster response. Designed for meteorologists, government disaster response authorities, and vulnerable civic infrastructures across Tamil Nadu, the visual aesthetic balances academic rigor with rapid emergency triage legibility.

Rooted in a sophisticated deep-ocean dark mode, the aesthetic conveys high-stakes analytical stability. Complex atmospheric simulations, oceanic dipole metrics, and predictive precipitation models are rendered through structural grid layouts, low-attenuation telemetry borders, and zero-latency visual indicators. The atmosphere is clinical, reliable, and deeply respectful of public safety protocols—avoiding sensationalism while delivering immediate situational clarity under high-pressure disaster scenarios.

## Colors

The palette leverages high-depth oceanic obsidian foundations to eliminate eye fatigue in 24/7 command center monitoring, offset by sharp spectral highlights for data visualization and early warning risk tiers.

- **Primary Canvas & Structural Fill (`#0A192F`):** Deep oceanic obsidian functioning as the base background canvas, creating an infinite contrast floor for overlays and spatial maps.
- **Secondary Structural Panels (`#0F2B5C`):** Sub-surface midnight navy applied across containment modules, telemetry sidebars, and analytical cards.
- **Teal Telemetry Accent (`#0D9488`):** Deep marine cyan used for sensor status, selected interactive states, active vectors, and primary operational triggers.
- **Neutral Typography & Structural Grid (`#94A3B8`):** Neutral slate providing calibrated legibility for dense tabular reading, metric labels, and structural hairpins. Pure white (`#F8FAFC`) is reserved for high-priority readouts and emergency headers.

### Tiered Disaster Alert Matrix
Warning statuses adhere to international meteorological standardization:
- **Low Risk (`#10B981`):** Calibrated emerald for baseline operations and normal climate models.
- **Moderate Risk (`#F59E0B`):** Amber for advisories, prolonged heat variations, and agricultural caution.
- **High Risk (`#F97316`):** Deep orange for severe precipitation warnings, coastal wave surges, and emergency readiness alerts.
- **Severe / Critical Alert (`#EF4444`):** Pure emergency red designated strictly for catastrophic flooding, cyclonic landfall, and rapid evacuation advisories.

## Typography

Typography pairs technical authority with effortless scanning under high cognitive loads.

- **IBM Plex Sans** drives all titles, numerical telemetry values, metrics, and risk tier badges. Its mechanical terminal angles and engineered letterforms convey industrial precision, eliminating ambiguities between numbers (e.g., 0 vs O, 1 vs I).
- **Hanken Grotesk** serves narrative context, system descriptions, meteorological briefings, and analytical body text. Its contemporary geometric proportions ensure fatigue-free comprehension across compact tables and responsive alert panels.
- Numerical measurements (such as rainfall anomalies in mm, sea surface temperature anomalies in °C, and barometric values in hPa) must always be formatted using tabular figures to maintain horizontal alignment in streaming dashboards.

## Layout & Spacing

The spatial engine utilizes a 12-column fluid grid system on desktop and tablet, collapsing to a single-column operational layout on mobile handhelds. Layout logic follows mission-critical hierarchy: primary geospatial telemetry maps receive structural priority, flanking telemetry cards provide live vector feeds, and analytical lower drawers render historical delta models.

- **Desktop (1280px+):** 12 columns, 24px gutters, 32px margins. Allows three-tier panel splitting (Navigation & Geospatial Selector, Central GIS Map & Temporal Slider, Right-Rail Alert Stream).
- **Tablet (768px - 1279px):** 8 columns, 16px gutters, 24px margins. Right-rail collates into bottom collapsible drawer.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns, 16px gutters, 16px margins. Stacked stream prioritized by warning urgency.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth in the system avoids organic light casting in favor of tonal atmospheric planes, precise physical containment lines, and chromatic illumination:

- **Surface Level 0 (Base Canvas):** `#0A192F` baseline, devoid of drop shadows.
- **Surface Level 1 (Analytical Cards & Modules):** `#0F2B5C` at 85% opacity over `#0A192F`, bounded by a 1px border of `rgba(148, 163, 184, 0.15)`.
- **Surface Level 2 (Floating Popovers & Overlays):** `#0B1B36` reinforced with a 1px border of `rgba(13, 148, 136, 0.35)` and an ambient shadow: `0 12px 24px -4px rgba(2, 6, 23, 0.65)`.
- **Warning Depth:** When an alert reaches High (`#F97316`) or Severe (`#EF4444`), containment borders accept a 1px chromatic halo with an outward ambient glow of `0 0 12px rgba(239, 68, 68, 0.25)`, ensuring instant focal capture in multi-monitor setups.

## Shapes

The interface embraces a precise, technical aesthetic driven by soft 4px (`roundedness: 1`) corner radii. This minimal rounding ensures geometric compactness across data grids and vector charts, avoiding casual softness while preventing harsh industrial corners that strain prolonged reading.

- Standard modules, metric cards, buttons, and alert strips leverage `0.25rem` (4px).
- Status pills, risk tags, and coordinate badges maintain `0.25rem` to retain structural alignment with enclosing tabular cells.
- Floating modal dialogs and alert drawers adopt `rounded-lg` (`0.5rem` / 8px).

## Components

### Buttons & Operational Triggers
- **Primary Action (Issue Advisory / Export Simulation):** Deep teal background (`#0D9488`), crisp white text (`#F8FAFC`), 4px radius, uppercase tracking (`0.04em`), height 36px. Hover state shifts to `#0F766E` with a 1px teal border glow.
- **Secondary / Telemetry Action:** Transparent background with a 1px border of `rgba(148, 163, 184, 0.25)`, text color `#94A3B8`. Hover transforms background to `rgba(15, 43, 92, 0.6)` and text to `#F8FAFC`.

### Risk & Early Warning Badges
- Strict color mapping matching the 4-tier risk matrix. 
- Formatted as a 20px height pill with uppercase font (`label-sm`), 1px solid border matching the tier color, and an internal tinted background at 12% opacity (e.g., Severe: background `rgba(239, 68, 68, 0.12)`, border `#EF4444`, text `#EF4444`).
- Critical/Severe status contains a pulsing 6px warning dot indicator on the leading edge.

### Data & Metric Cards
- Background `#0F2B5C` (or 80% opacity equivalent), 1px border of `rgba(148, 163, 184, 0.12)`.
- Header section uses `label-md` in `#94A3B8` accompanied by telemetry sensor metadata.
- Metric values utilize `telemetry-data` in `#F8FAFC` accompanied by contextual trend indicators (delta arrows).

### Form Controls & Filter Selectors
- **Input Fields & District Dropdowns:** Background `#0A192F`, border 1px `rgba(148, 163, 184, 0.2)`, text `#F8FAFC`, placeholder `#64748B`. Focused state applies a 1px border of `#0D9488` with no offset outline.
- **Checkboxes & Segmented Radios:** Geometric squares with 2px radius, fill `#0A192F`, active state filled with `#0D9488` containing an optical white checkmark.

### Telemetry Status Chips
- Compact, non-intrusive metadata capsules for climate satellite feeds (e.g., "INSAT-3DR: LIVE", "IOD INDEX: +0.42"). Bordered with muted slate (`rgba(148, 163, 184, 0.2)`), displaying monospaced numerical status markers.
